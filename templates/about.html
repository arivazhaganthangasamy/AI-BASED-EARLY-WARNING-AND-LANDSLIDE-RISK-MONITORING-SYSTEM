About the system

Landslides often follow heavy rain and saturated soil, and warning signs appear hours or days earlier. This project turns field readings into an early warning that people can act on.

How it works

  1. Enter site readingsRainfall, soil moisture, slope angle, ground movement, temperature, groundwater level and landslide history.
  2. The model estimates probabilityAn optimized Random Forest returns the chance of a landslide.
  3. Risk is classifiedLow, Medium, High or Critical, using thresholds saved with the model.
  4. People are warnedAn alarm sounds in the browser and a Telegram message goes to the response team.
  5. Results are loggedEach check is stored and shown on the dashboard, with a CSV download.

Technology

Python, Flask, scikit-learn Random Forest, Telegram Bot API, Chart.js.

Site photos

Photos are saved with the record for reference. The model predicts from the seven numeric readings, not from image pixels.

Limits

This tool supports decisions and does not replace expert geotechnical assessment or official warnings.
